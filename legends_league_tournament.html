How it runs

Four stages. Straight to a final.

Every team is built to be competitive on paper before a ball is hit — the drama comes from what happens on court, not from a lopsided draw.

01

Seed

Confirmed attendees are paired off the live ladder — top player with the lowest-ranked attendee, and so on toward the middle — so every doubles team carries roughly the same combined strength.

02

Groups

Teams are split into small groups and play multiple round-robin matches against different opponents. Everyone gets real court time before anyone is out.

03

Knockout

Top finishers from each group advance into a straight elimination bracket — quarterfinals, semifinals, one match at a time.

04

Final

Two teams, one match, one Legends League Tournament One champion. Everything else is a warm-up for this.

The trophy is real.
